DS-00

Overview

Design principles

Two audiences: older patients in a corridor with 60 seconds of patience, and a chief doctor who needs to see what is on fire in 5 seconds.

01

One tap per decision

Ratings auto-advance. No screen asks for two things at once.

02

Calm, not clinical

Off-white surfaces, one teal accent. Red only means "a patient is unhappy".

03

Numbers first

The dashboard leads with KPIs and unanswered complaints; charts come second.

04

Readable at arm's length

Patient text ≥ 16px, actions ≥ 56px, contrast ≥ 4.5:1 everywhere.

05

Promise only what we keep

No "24 h" deadlines in copy until the center commits to them.

06

Tokens or nothing

A raw hex, px size or shadow outside ds.css is a bug.

General usage rules

Applies to every screen in the product.

  • Light theme only; dark is out of scope for v1.
  • One primary button per view.
  • Patient screens are mobile-first (360–430px).
  • Dashboard is desktop-first (≥ 1200px) but must work on a phone.
  • Motion explains change; prefers-reduced-motion turns it off.
  • Every changing number uses tabular figures.
  • Patient phone numbers are always masked in the dashboard.
  • Three scripts must fit at 360px: Ўзбекча, Oʻzbekcha, Qaraqalpaqsha.

Writing rules

Copy is part of the design. Patients read it standing up, under stress.

Voice

  • Polite "siz / сиз" form.
  • One idea per sentence, one question per screen.
  • Dashboard: neutral, never shames staff.

Buttons & states

  • Verbs: "Davom etish", "Yuborish", "Javob yuborish".
  • Empty states say what happened: "Hammasiga javob berilgan ✓".
  • Errors say how to fix, not just "Xato".

Scripts & numbers

  • Latin Uzbek: oʻ gʻ (U+02BB), ʼ (U+02BC).
  • Karakalpak reviewed by a native speaker.
  • Tabular figures, 24h time, masked phones.
DS-03

Foundations

Spacing · 4px grid

The only allowed padding, margin and gap values. Space inside a group is smaller than space between groups.

Radius

Concentric rule: outer = inner + padding (segmented track 14 = item 10 + 4).

Elevation

Borders separate, shadows lift. Shadows are transparent ink, never black.

--sh-1resting cards, secondary button
--sh-2raised, dropdowns
--sh-3phone, poster, toast
--sh-accentprimary button only

Motion

Hover a track to play it. Easing --ease-out; reduced motion sets every duration to 0.
